Written Summative Examination Structure

Official question types, time division, and marks composition

3 Hours Duration

90 Marks Written Examination

20 Mins MCQ + SBA (20 Marks)
2h 40m SAQ + SEQ + EQ (70 Marks)
  • Group A + Group B Structure: Question paper consists of Group A (35 marks) and Group B (35 marks). Each group answered on separate scripts.
  • MCQ (True/False): 1 stem with 5 alternatives (0.2 mark each = 1 mark per question). Ratio 3:2 or 2:3.
  • Single Best Answer (SBA): 1 stem with 4 positive options. 1 mark per question.
  • Question Cognitive Composition:
    Recall 60% Analytical 20% Problem 20%
  • No Negative Marking: Wrong answers incur zero penalty. Fractions ≥0.5 rounded up to 1.
2h 30m Duration

60 Marks Written Examination

20 Mins MCQ + SBA (20 Marks)
2h 10m SAQ + SEQ (40 Marks)
  • Applicable Courses: B111 Communicative English, B112 Nursing Informatics, B224 Pharmacology, B237 Laws & Ethics, B433 Administration, B416 Advanced English.
  • English Specific Format: Fill in the blanks & correct answers used instead of traditional True/False stems.
  • Group Division: Group A = 20 marks + Group B = 20 marks. Separate answer scripts used for each group.
  • Question Options: Answer 2 SAQs out of 3, and 1 SEQ out of 2 per group.
  • Pass Criteria: Minimum 60% marks required across the combined written theory paper.

Oral & Practical/Clinical Assessment Methods

Conducted immediately after written examinations using standardized SOE and OSPE stations

SOE System

Structured Oral Examination (SOE)

Conducted with both Internal and External examiners present. Questions are drawn randomly from 5 distinct boxes:

3 Boxes Recall Questions 15–20 Qs per box
1 Box Analytical 10–15 Qs
1 Box Problem-Based 10–15 Qs
  • Evaluation: Both internal and external examiners score each question independently, and the arithmetic mean/average is awarded.
  • Time per Student: 10 to 25 minutes depending on the course mark allocation. Max 20 examinees per day.
OSPE System

Objective Structured Practical Exam (OSPE)

Practical competencies are assessed using two station categories:

🔍 Spotting Station 3–4 Mins per Station

Instrument identification, clinical usage, and sterilization methods.

🩺 Procedure Station 5–10 Mins per Task

Directly performing nursing procedures (e.g. BP measurement, sterile dressing) evaluated against checklist.

  • Exclusions: OSPE is not conducted for Communicative English, Informatics, Pharmacology, Laws & Ethics, Administration, and Research.
  • Clinical Log: Reflective diary and practical notebook contribute up to 5 marks in core practical courses.

Academic Retake & Promotion Policy

• Course-Specific Retake: If a student fails in an individual course (e.g., Anatomy), they are only required to re-appear for that specific course.

• 3 Standard Chances: Students have three chances to pass 1st-year courses. They may attend 2nd-year classes, but cannot sit for 2nd-year final examinations until all 1st-year courses are passed.

• 4th & 5th Attempts: A 4th attempt requires special permission from the Dean (with 1-year drop). A 5th attempt requires permission from the Vice Chancellor (allowed only once in the entire 4-year program).

• Registration Validity: B.Sc. in Nursing registration remains valid for a maximum of 8 years.
